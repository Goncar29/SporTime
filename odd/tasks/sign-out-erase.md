Objective: when someone signs out, send their pending changes first, then erase that account's data from the device; if the changes cannot be sent, warn before erasing.
Branch: feat/sign-out-erase, from main at 2b4a038. Delivery strategy: ask-on-risk; push, merge and deploy only with the user's OK. Test runner: `npx pnpm@10 exec vp test run`, `npx pnpm@10 exec vp check`, plus the Chrome and emulator end-to-end suites.

## Specs

- S1. Question: "¿Borramos los datos que quedan en el dispositivo al cerrar sesión (la caché de Firestore y los cambios pendientes de envío)?" Answer: "Sí, pero sin perder datos" — "Al cerrar sesión, primero se envían los cambios pendientes. Si no hay conexión, se avisa antes de borrar. Es un cambio con tests."
  1. Signing out first sends every pending change of that account: the app's own outbox (localStorage `sportime.outbox.workouts.<uid>`, `sportime.outbox.history.<uid>`) and Firestore's queued writes (`waitForPendingWrites`, before Firebase `signOut`, with a time limit because it never settles offline).
  2. Once everything is sent, the app signs out and erases that account's local data: the Firestore IndexedDB cache (`terminate` then `clearIndexedDbPersistence`) and both outbox keys. The page then reloads so a fresh Firestore starts.
  3. Data already on the server is never touched: after signing back in, every workout and history entry is still there.
  4. Device settings stay: `sportime.language`, `sportime.soundSettings`, `sportime.installDismissedAt`.
- S2. Question: what to do offline with unsent changes. Answer: "Avisar y dejar elegir" — "Un aviso como 'Tenés cambios sin enviar. Si cerrás sesión ahora se pierden.' con dos botones: 'Quedarme' o 'Cerrar sesión igual' (borra todo)."
  1. Spanish copy, exactly: message "Tienes cambios sin enviar. Si cierras sesión ahora, se perderán.", buttons "Quedarme" and "Cerrar sesión igual".
  2. English copy: "You have unsent changes. If you sign out now, they will be lost.", buttons "Stay" and "Sign out anyway".
  3. "Quedarme"/"Stay" closes the warning and keeps the session and the pending changes as they were; they are sent when the connection returns, as today.
  4. "Cerrar sesión igual"/"Sign out anyway" discards the pending changes, signs out and erases as in S1.2.
  5. While sending, the menu shows that it is signing out ("Signing out…" / "Cerrando sesión…") and the button cannot be pressed twice.
- S3. "Hacerlo completo": implement the whole behavior with tests and separate commits, after this scope was explained: forcing the send, shutting down Firestore and clearing its cache with a reload, other open tabs, the warning and the privacy text.
  1. Other open tabs of the app: when they see the account sign out, they reload, so none keeps a stopped Firestore or an old outbox in memory.
  2. The privacy screen's "On this device" paragraph (`en.ts`, `es.ts`) stops saying data stays after signing out and says it is erased when signing out.
  3. The emulator e2e test "another account's unsent writes survive when Bob … signs out" (`e2e-emulator/account.spec.ts`) describes the old behavior; it is replaced by tests of S1 and S2.
- Assumptions: "sent" means the server confirmed it (outbox empty and `waitForPendingWrites` resolved), not just written to the local cache. Time limit for sending: 10 seconds, then the S2 warning. Clearing the Firestore cache clears it for the whole browser profile; any other account's unsent writes were already sent or discarded at its own sign-out. In the e2e fake mode (no Firestore) erasing is a no-op and sign-out stays as today. Sign-outs that the provider forces (expired session, account deletion) keep their current behavior.

- S4. Question: "¿Cómo protegemos los cambios de otra pestaña mientras una pestaña cierra sesión?" Answer: "Candado entre pestañas (Web Locks) (recomendado)" — "Mientras A cierra sesión (hasta ~10 s): B muestra \"Cerrando sesión en otra pestaña…\" y queda bloqueada. Un guardado que justo llega en B espera a que A termine: si A no cerró (sin conexión / Quedarme), se guarda normal; si A cerró, no se borra en silencio: B avisa \"No se pudo guardar: se cerró la sesión en otra pestaña.\" y recarga al login. Si el navegador no tiene Web Locks: se mantiene el comportamiento actual (A5 queda como límite)."
  1. While tab A signs out, every other open tab of the same account shows "Cerrando sesión en otra pestaña…" (English: "Signing out in another tab…") and its screens cannot be used; when A ends without signing out (warning shown, "Quedarme", provider refused), they become usable again.
  2. A change started in another tab during that time waits until A finishes. If A did not sign out, it is saved as usual. If A signed out, it is never erased silently: that tab shows "No se pudo guardar: se cerró la sesión en otra pestaña." (English: "Couldn't save: you signed out in another tab.") and then reloads to the sign-in screen.
  3. No change made in any tab is erased unless the server confirmed it (S1 "sin perder datos", now across tabs).
  4. Browsers without Web Locks keep the behavior from T1-T4.
- S4 assumptions: the English copy is a translation of the user's Spanish text. A finished timer that records history in another tab counts as a change (S4.2). The lock is per account and released by the browser if a tab closes or crashes.

- S5. User: "podemos publicar pero tratemos de resolver los pendientes" (answer: publish "Después de resolver los pendientes (recomendado)"). The pending items are the ones listed after T5 (L15) and the T5 review findings:
  1. Forced sign-out without the lock (R1-force-bypasses-lock, R3-force-fallback-unlocked): when another tab does not let go within 10 s and the user chose "Sign out anyway", a change waiting in that other tab is never lost silently: that tab shows the S4.2 message. Pinned by a test.
  2. A change waiting for the lock lives only in memory: while one waits, closing or reloading that tab asks the browser's leave-page confirmation, so it is not lost silently. The app's own reloads (sign-out, the S4.2 message reload) never ask.
  3. Own tab (R4-own-tab-guarded-write-refused): a change requested in the signing-out tab itself while it holds the lock (for example a workout that finishes) is never lost after the reload: the sign-out reports "unsent" and the S2 warning shows, and the change is then saved.
  4. The two-tab test in signOutDataSafety.test.ts pins its outcome (R3-cross-tab-outcome-unpinned).
  5. A real-browser emulator test of S4.2: a change made in another tab during the window is saved on the server when the signing-out tab stays, and is refused with the S4.2 message (nothing new on the server, nothing left on the device) when it signs out.
  6. Review suggestions: the "signing out" waiter in otherTabs.ts handles a rejected lock (R3-signing-out-waiter-unhandled); the updateDoc mock no longer leaks between tests (R3-updateDoc-mock-leak).
  - Not in scope: the L13 #7 residual stays a note.

- S6. Question (parent, after T6): a change saved in another tab while the "unsent changes" warning shows, then discarded by "Sign out anyway", is lost without a message in that tab. Recommendation accepted by the user ("bien haremos lo que recomiendas"): that tab warns too, with the existing S4.2 message.
  1. When the account signs out from another tab and this tab holds changes it stored that the server has not confirmed (for example saved while the other tab showed the S2 warning, then discarded by "Cerrar sesión igual"/"Sign out anyway"), this tab shows "No se pudo guardar: se cerró la sesión en otra pestaña." / "Couldn't save: you signed out in another tab." and then reloads to sign-in, as in S4.2. When every change it made was confirmed, it reloads at once as today.
  2. The signing-out tab's flow and its S2 warning do not change.
