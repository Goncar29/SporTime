Objective: users keep training routines split into stages in a new Profile section, and share them with saved contacts (phases 2-4).
Branch: feat/routines, from main. Delivery strategy: work-unit commits on the branch; rules publish, push, merge and deploy only with the user's OK (rules before the app). Test runner: `npx pnpm@10 exec vp test run`; rules with `test:rules` on the emulator; emulator end-to-end suite.

## Specs

- S1. Routine content: "una seccion donde un usuario pueda cargar sus rutinas de entrenamientos dividia en pequeñas secciones por etapas con posible espacio de comentarios aparte si es deseado, donde se pueda poner en cada etapa el nombre de entrenamiento, repeticiones por (ejemplo 8 - 10), pausas en tiempo (ejemplo 90') y el peso".
- S2. Lifecycle and place: "estas rutinas se puedan crear, editar, eliminar y compartir con otros usuarios en una seccion nueva dentro del perfil".
- S3. Optional fields: "los valores a agruegar en esas secciones de ejercicios son opcionales, sin obligacion que se deba de llenar todos los campos". Accepted limits ("acepto los limites que dices"): the routine needs a name; an exercise with no field filled is discarded on save; a stage without a name shows as "Etapa 1", "Etapa 2"…
- S4. Field shapes (accepted plan): reps empty, one value ("10") or a range ("8 - 10") stored as `repsMin`/`repsMax`; rest stored as `restSeconds` and shown like 90''; weight is a number plus unit, "Número + unidad (kg/lb)", unit required only when a weight is present (default kg).
- S5. Storage: Firestore, no new technology ("Sin servidor por ahora"). Works offline through the existing outbox; no routine is lost when a write fails or the device is offline. Security rules validate every field; text is rendered as text only (never as HTML).
- S6. Account: deleting the account and signing out erase routines like workouts and history.
- S7. Sharing (phases 2-4): "la manera de compartir podria ser agregar el email del usuario a una lista, cuando un usuario quiera compartir esa rutina se pueda seleccionar y elegir el/los email guardados y que muestre en esa lista la foto de la persona y seleccionarlos, al agregar se pueda confirmar el envio y que estos usuario los reciban en su perfil recibiendolo con una notificacion y se guarden en su perfil." Decisions: received routines "Se guarda automáticamente"; senders: "Solo quien me tiene en contactos"; in-app notification only; block sender.

- S9. Links: "que se pueda agregar un link en cada ejerciocio para que donde si existe un link se pueda abrir con un boton cuando alguien quiera ir a ese link" — optional, https only, opened by a button.
- S10. View layout: the link is a button at the end of the exercise row that "solo diga ver"; reps, rest, weight and the button sit to the right, not "dividido en 4 partes iguales".
- S11. Contacts and directory (phase 2), user decisions: directory "Opt-in: solo quien lo activa en el Perfil"; contacts show "Nombre y foto de Google"; at most "50" contacts per user. Accepted plan: a Profile switch "Permitir que me encuentren por email" writes or deletes `directory/{normalized email}` (uid, name, photo); only the signed-in owner of that verified email writes it; anyone signed in may read one exact email (get), never list. A user who is not opted in cannot be added. Photos only from googleusercontent.com, otherwise the name's initial. Contacts live in `users/{uid}/contacts/{contactUid}` (owner only, through the outbox, work offline); add by email, list with photo, remove. Account deletion erases contacts and the directory entry; sign-out erases this device's copy only, and the server directory entry stays until the switch is turned off (L21).
- S8. Viewing: "despues pueda visualizarla como si fuese una tabla con la informacion dentro" — a read-only view of a routine, opened from the list, one table per stage.
- S12. Sharing and receiving (phases 3-4), user decisions (L48): "Desarrollar 3 y 4, publicar juntas"; what is sent is "Una copia independiente" — saved as the recipient's own routine marked "Recibida de <nombre>"; later edits by the sender do not change it, and sending again delivers another copy; links to localhost or IP addresses (e.g. https://192.168.0.1) are "Rechazarlos en todas las rutinas" (own and received; local network names too); the sender is shown with "Nombre, foto y email de Google del que envía", taken by the rules from the sender's Google sign-in so it cannot be forged; nothing from anyone's contacts (names, photos) is ever sent. Accepted plan: the sender picks a routine, then one or more saved contacts (photo list), confirms, and a copy goes to `users/{recipient}/inbox/{id}`; the rules allow it only when the sender has the recipient in their contacts (S7 "Solo quien me tiene en contactos"), validate the whole routine strictly (L7 A1: no blank names, no reversed reps, no empty lines) and never allow updating it. The recipient's app moves each inbox copy into their routines ("Se guarda automáticamente", through the outbox) and deletes it from the inbox. Phase 4: an in-app notice of new received routines and blocking a sender, after which the rules refuse that sender's copies.
